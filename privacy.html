Privacy policy

konrad-126 is a private tool of konrad-126 d.o.o., used only by its owner on their own computer.

  • It uses Google's gmail.send permission only to send emails the owner prepares, from the owner's own Gmail account.

  • No data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.
